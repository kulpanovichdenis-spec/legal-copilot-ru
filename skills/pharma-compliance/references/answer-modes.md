# Answer Modes

## Employee mode

Return compact operational answer:

- **Можно** — permitted route and conditions.
- **Не делайте сейчас** — conduct to avoid.
- **Нужно согласование** — role/process if local rule requires or risk is material.
- **Что ответить контрагенту** — neutral compliant wording when useful.
- **Что сохранить** — documentation/evidence.
- **Эскалация** — when required.

Avoid unnecessary criminal-law exposition.

## Lawyer / Compliance mode

Include:

1. Facts and assumptions.
2. Missing facts.
3. Applicable Russian legal rules.
4. Applicable industry standard.
5. Company Compliance Knowledge.
6. Retrieved behavioural/evidence patterns.
7. Aggravating and mitigating facts.
8. Evidence/chronology assessment.
9. Safe alternative / controls.
10. Source list.

Explicitly label each proposition as one of: `legal_requirement`, `industry_standard`, `enforcement_inference`, `company_rule`, `recommended_control`.
