# Scope

## In scope

Conduct / integrity / interaction / influence / conflict / competition / corruption / enforcement risks in pharmaceutical and medtech business interactions.

## Out of scope by default

Drug registration, GMP, manufacturing, product quality, PV, recalls, routine regulatory submissions and other product regulation.

## Misconduct crossover

Out-of-scope regulatory processes enter this playbook when facts indicate concealment, falsification, improper influence, corruption, conflicts, deliberate circumvention of controls, retaliation or other integrity misconduct.
