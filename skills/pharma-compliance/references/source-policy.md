# Source Policy

## 1. Russian legal conclusions

Use in this order where available:

1. legislation / official legal text;
2. Constitutional Court / Supreme Court / court act;
3. official prosecutor/regulator/enforcement material;
4. applicable authoritative industry standard (e.g. AIPM).

## 2. Foreign materials

DOJ, SEC, OIG, official court records, settlement documents and CIAs may support behavioural/evidentiary patterns. They do not establish Russian legal rules.

## 3. Discovery sources

Presentations, law-firm reviews, journals, blogs and media can generate leads. Before adding a final authority object, search for an authoritative source. If unavailable, mark `secondary_only` and do not use it as the primary basis for a legal conclusion.

## 4. Source labeling

Each source must include jurisdiction, source type, authority level, URL, publisher, relevant date and status.

## 5. Current law

A retrieved legal text must be checked for version/effective date when the question is date-sensitive.
