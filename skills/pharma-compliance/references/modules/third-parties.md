# Module — Third Parties

## Actors

Distributors, agents, consultants, event agencies, CROs and other intermediaries.

## Analyse

- business rationale;
- due diligence;
- compensation / commission / success fee;
- scope and deliverables;
- contacts with HCP/HCO/public officials;
- indirect benefits;
- unusual payment routing;
- documentation;
- monitoring;
- red flags and response.

Third-party form does not break the causal chain. Distinguish `payee`, `intermediary` and `ultimate_beneficiary`.
