# Module — Investigations / Evidence / Reporting

## Coverage

Internal reports, investigation triggers, preservation, electronic communications, expense records, false/retrospective documentation, concealment and remediation.

## Principle

The product is preventive, not a tool for concealing evidence. It should help create a truthful, contemporaneous evidence trail of lawful business purpose, approvals and actual performance.

## Questions

- What control should have operated?
- Did it operate?
- What evidence proves that?
- What chronology exists?
- Are documents contemporaneous or retrospective?
- Is expense/accounting description consistent with actual activity?
