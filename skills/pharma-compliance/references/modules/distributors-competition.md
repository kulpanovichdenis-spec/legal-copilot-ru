# Module — Distributors / Commercial Practices / Competition

## Boundary

A distributor acting in a public tender may trigger `PUBLIC_PROCUREMENT`. Ordinary manufacturer-distributor commercial restraints and information flows belong here.

## Coverage

Resale price restrictions, discounts/rebates/bonuses, exclusivity, territories/customers, non-compete, parallel distribution, commercially sensitive information, distributor-to-distributor coordination, hub-and-spoke, market/customer allocation and bid coordination.

## Information provenance

Always ask: what information, whose information, who supplied it, why the company possessed it, sensitivity, who received it and intended use.

## Guardrail

Do not infer collusion from mere possession or ordinary vertical communication. Look for coordination, intended market effect and role of the manufacturer as conduit/facilitator.
