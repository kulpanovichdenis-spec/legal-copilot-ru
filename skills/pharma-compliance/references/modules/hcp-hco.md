# Module — HCP / HCO Interactions

## Coverage

Speaker programs, consulting, advisory boards, scientific/educational events, congress support, travel/accommodation/meals, grants, donations, sponsorship, research relationships, samples/free goods/equipment, prescribing/formulary/guideline influence and conflicts.

## Required questions

- What is the legitimate purpose?
- Was the need defined before recipient selection?
- Why this HCP/HCO?
- What value/benefit is transferred and to whom?
- Is there a real service/deliverable?
- Is remuneration FMV/reasonable?
- Can recipient influence prescribing, formulary, procurement, guidelines or other company-relevant decisions?
- Is any explicit or implicit linkage present?
- What documentary evidence exists?
- What Company Compliance Knowledge applies?

## Output principle

Do not equate payment with misconduct. Identify the lawful route and the facts that move the engagement toward disguised inducement.
