# Module — Public Procurement / State Healthcare

## Routes

### Direct
`manufacturer → public customer`

### Through distributor
`manufacturer → distributor → public procurement → HCO`

## Analyse

Pre-tender contacts; non-public procurement information; needs assessment; technical specifications; NMCK/commercial proposals; competitor quotations; tender/bid support; special tender price; acceptance; complaints; contract changes.

## Distributor route attribution questions

- What did the distributor do?
- What did the manufacturer know?
- What did the manufacturer request, approve or facilitate?
- What pricing/incentives were provided?
- Were there unusual requests or red flags?
- Did the manufacturer continue support after red flags?

## Interaction scale

`INFORMATION → CONSULTATION → INFLUENCE → COORDINATION → MANIPULATION`

Objective product information is not treated as equivalent to restrictive specification drafting. The result must explain where the facts sit on the scale and why.
