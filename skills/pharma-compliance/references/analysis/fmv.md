# Analysis — Fair Market Value (FMV)

FMV is a structured assessment, not a magic safe-harbor number.

## Minimum fields

Service type, expertise, preparation/delivery time, benchmark method/range, agreed amount, deviation and reason, approver, date.

## Rules

- Do not infer that a fee is acceptable solely because it falls below a universal threshold.
- Do not infer that a fee is improper solely because it is high in absolute terms.
- Examine service substance, expertise, time, benchmark method and decision influence.
- Document material deviations from the benchmark.
- Company Compliance Knowledge may impose stricter methodology, caps or approvals.
