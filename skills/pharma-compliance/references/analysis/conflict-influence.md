# Analysis — Conflict / Influence

Do not classify a person merely as `HCP`. Record decision roles: prescriber, department head, formulary influence, procurement influence, acceptance influence, guideline influence, regulator/public role.

## Core question

Can the recipient's personal/material interest influence or appear to influence a professional or public decision material to the company?

Use this dimension together with transfer-of-value and evidence analysis; decision-role alone does not prove misconduct.
