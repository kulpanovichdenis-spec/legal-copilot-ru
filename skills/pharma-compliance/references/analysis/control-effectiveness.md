# Analysis — Control Effectiveness

## Model

`Risk → Control design → Implementation → Evidence of operation → Failure/remediation`.

Policy existence is not equivalent to operating effectiveness.

Possible evidence: assigned/complete training, knowledge test, documented pre-approval, FMV assessment, escalation record, monitoring result, investigation, remediation/discipline.

Do not state that training or a policy automatically eliminates corporate liability. Treat controls as facts relevant to prevention, detection, response and evidence of compliance efforts.
