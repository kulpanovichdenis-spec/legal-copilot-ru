# Analysis — Transfer of Value

Apply to fees, consulting, speaker honoraria, travel, accommodation, meals, conference fees, grants, donations, equipment, free use, research funding, clinical-trial payments, sponsorship and other benefits.

## Test

1. **Purpose** — independent lawful/scientific/business purpose?
2. **Need** — genuine need defined before selection?
3. **Selection** — objective reason for this recipient?
4. **Value / FMV** — reasonable and supportable?
5. **Performance** — real service/output delivered?
6. **Influence** — recipient can affect prescribing/formulary/procurement/etc.?
7. **Linkage** — direct/indirect connection between value and decision?
8. **Documentation** — contemporaneous evidence?
9. **Concealment** — label/accounting/category matches substance?

## Risk transition

Legitimate professional engagement becomes materially riskier when value is selected, timed, increased or continued because of a professional decision favorable to the company, especially where service is weak/fictitious or documentation masks substance.
